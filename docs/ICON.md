Two bold, friendly illustrated glass beer steins filled with rich amber beer and topped with white foam, gently tilted toward each other in a toast. Clean polished illustration, simple strong silhouettes and restrained dimensional shading, highly legible at small home-screen icon sizes. Center the two steins together with enough safe margin for iOS corner masking; both handles and both labels fully visible. Full-bleed solid deep teal background for contrast. Square 1024x1024 composition, opaque background, no border, no pre-rounded corners, no surrounding device mockup, no watermark.
